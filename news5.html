iPhone 17 Air is more than its slim profile: 5 expected high-end features

The iPhone 17 Air is being applauded for its slim profile, according to the latest leaks and rumours. But there is more to it. Here are 5 high-end features (rumoured) that prove that the iPhone 17 Air is no less than a pro.

The iPhone 17 Air's leaked images have taken the internet by storm, sparking excitement among tech enthusiasts. The latest leaks have highlighted the slim profile of the upcoming iPhone 17 Air. Thanks to its 5.65mm thickness, a hands-on video claims it to be as thin as a pencil. While it is true that Apple has plans to compromise a few features to cater to the slim profile, rumour has it that the iPhone 17 Air will be no less than a pro. In this article, let's focus on the upcoming high-end features, which are rumoured to make the iPhone 17 Air even better.

iPhone 17 Air: 5 key upcoming features

12GB RAM: Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo has recently suggested that Apple may equip both the iPhone 17 Air and iPhone 17 Pro with identical RAM specifications — 12GB. This move, if true, marks a departure from the company’s usual practice of reserving increased memory for the top-tier Pro Max model. This feature will boost the iPhone 17 Air's performance, which could deliver a flagship-level experience, despite its seemingly less premium branding within the range. Reports indicate that Apple is re-evaluating its strategy due to evolving demands for AI capabilities and multitasking performance.

Battery: Ever since the iPhone 17 Air's slim profile came into the picture, the concerns regarding the battery have been quite popular. However, previous reports have suggested that Apple is making sure that the iPhone will pull off impressive battery specs, especially with respect to its thinness. Apple is reportedly working on two major innovations to ensure the iPhone 17 Air maintains strong battery life, despite its ultra-slim design. One involves a complete redesign of the display and internal silicon to boost energy efficiency, while the other centres on the introduction of high-density battery cells, capable of delivering greater power in a smaller footprint.

Apple's in-house 5G modem: Another notable change could lie beneath the surface. The iPhone 17 Air is anticipated to feature Apple’s own 5G modem, replacing the current solution supplied by Qualcomm. This would make it the second iPhone to utilise Apple’s in-house modem, following the iPhone 16e (review). The custom-designed modem is reportedly capable of download speeds reaching up to 4Gbps, although it may lack support for mmWave 5G. In addition, the iPhone 17 Air is expected to feature Apple’s new custom-built C1 modem, which is designed to cut down on power usage during 5G connectivity—tackling a persistent issue that users have faced with battery drain while on mobile networks.

A19 chipset: When it comes to performance, the iPhone 17 Air is expected to feature Apple’s standard A19 chipset. Although the Pro variants are likely to receive the more advanced A19 Pro chip, the regular A19 is still anticipated to deliver impressive performance suitable for the vast majority of users. This approach may allow Apple to keep the iPhone 17 Air at a more accessible price point, appealing to those who seek a sleek and capable iPhone without opting for the high-end Pro range.

120Hz ProMotion display: Apple plans to introduce 120Hz ProMotion displays across the entire iPhone 17 range, including the iPhone 17 Air, not just the Pro editions. Paired with Always-On Display functionality, this enhancement is expected to significantly improve overall smoothness and responsiveness, whether you’re scrolling through apps or playing games. In addition, rumours point to the introduction of a new anti-reflective and scratch-resistant coating on all models. This upgrade would not only boost screen clarity in bright conditions but also offer greater durability, reducing the reliance on external screen protectors.

iPhone 17 Air: India price (expected)

Apple has yet to confirm any official pricing for the iPhone 17 Air, but a report from Tom’s Guide suggests the company may aim to keep the base price in line with the current iPhone 16 Plus. If that proves accurate, the iPhone 17Air's price could start at $899 in the US and Rs 89,900 in India. However, other sources indicate that the price could exceed even that of the iPhone 16 Pro Max, which currently begins at $1,199 in the US and Rs 1,44,900 in India. On a broader scale, global trade dynamics may also influence pricing. The US government recently granted temporary tariff exemptions on electronics like smartphones, potentially allowing Apple to avoid passing on additional costs from China-based production. However, with the US elections approaching and trade policies in flux, the final pricing of the iPhone 17 lineup remains uncertain.
